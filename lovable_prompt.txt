Build a production-grade, Apple Human Interface Design (HIG) Dental Clinical & Forensic Platform named "DentalSafe TCLE AI" for dentists, implantologists, and facial aesthetics (HOF) professionals.

### Tech Stack & Architecture:
- Framework: Vite + React 18 + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + Lucide React + Radix/Shadcn UI
- Storage: LocalStorage by default (Zero-build client-side resilience) + Supabase client integration
- Design System: Apple Cupertino/Pro dark & light mode, frosted glass (backdrop-blur), SF Pro typography, refined gradients, tactile pill badges.

### Core Modules & Dedicated Tabs:

1. TAB "PACIENTES" (LGPD Patient Management):
   - Comprehensive CRUD for dental patients: Full Name, CPF (with validation mask), RG, Phone/WhatsApp (with click-to-chat link), Date of Birth (automatic age calculation), Email, Address, Drug Allergies/Alerts (red pill badge), Clinical Notes.
   - Quick Actions:
     * "Iniciar TCLE": Pre-fills generator form with patient data and navigates to TCLE generator.
     * "Nova Receita": Pre-fills prescription with patient data and navigates to Rx module.
     * "Dossiê LGPD (JSON)": Exports complete portability record under LGPD Art. 18.
     * "Anonimizar LGPD": Irreversibly replaces personal identifiable info (PII) with hash token while preserving clinical data for legal defense (LGPD Art. 16, I).
   - Search bar filtering by name, CPF, or phone in real-time.
   - Quick stats cards: Total Patients, Linked TCLEs, Issued Prescriptions, LGPD Compliance (100%).

2. TAB "GERADOR DE TCLE" (4-Step Forensic Consent Wizard):
   - Step 1: Patient Data & Legal Status (Adult, Minor with Guardian, PCD).
   - Step 2: Dental Procedure (21 procedures: Implants, Sisos, Endo, Bone Graft, Sinus Lift, HOF, Clear Aligners, etc.) + Interactive Odontogram with 32 teeth selection.
   - Step 3: Anesthesia Safety Selector (Articaine, Mepivacaine, Lidocaine with adrenaline contraindications check) + Comorbidities (Diabetes, Bisphosphonates/BRONJ, Anticoagulants).
   - Step 4: Live Dual Document Preview (Forensic Legal Text + Accessible Lay Summary in plain Portuguese) + Interactive Signature Canvas for patient and dentist + PDF Print with SHA-256 forensic hash stamp.

3. TAB "RECEITAS & ATESTADOS" (Pharmacological Rx & Certificates):
   - 41 Dental Drugs Catalog categorized into: AINEs (Ibuprofeno, Nimesulida, Cetorolaco, etc.), Analgésicos (Dipirona, Paracetamol, Tramadol), Antibióticos (Amoxicilina, Clavulanato, Azitromicina, Clindamicina), Tópicos (Clorexidina 0,12%, Omcilon-A, Nistatina) e Corticoides (Dexametasona, Betametasona).
   - Quick Clinical Presets: Oral Surgery / Sisos, Implant & Graft, Acute Pulpitis, AHA Endocarditis Prophylaxis, Penicillin-Allergic.
   - Dual-mode editing: Add/Remove drug chips OR edit directly in free-form paper sheet with instant sync.
   - Medical Leave (Atestado de Repouso com cálculo de dias por extenso) and Attendance Certificate (Declaração de Comparecimento com horários e Art. 473 CLT).
   - Export to WhatsApp and Timbrada Print layout.

4. TAB "HARMONIZAÇÃO OROFACIAL (HOF PRO)":
   - 5 Facial Danger Zones (Glabella, Alar base, Lips, Temporal, Mandibular) with anatomical artery mappings and forensic risk clauses.
   - DeLorenzi SOS Vascular Occlusion Rescue Protocol (minute-by-minute high dose hyaluronidase guide).
   - Image rights addendum compliant with Resolução CFO-196/2019.

5. TAB "INTERCORRÊNCIAS CLÍNICAS (SOS)":
   - 27 Emergency Protocols (Paresthesia, Broken needle, Alveolitis, Sinus perforation, Anaphylaxis, etc.).

6. TOP BAR ERGONOMICS:
   - Light/Dark theme toggle.
   - "Modo Consultório LGPD": Blurs all sensitive patient data on screen (CPF, phone, diagnosis) to prevent shoulder surfing in clinic.

Seed with realistic dental mock data so the app runs fully interactive on the first render.
